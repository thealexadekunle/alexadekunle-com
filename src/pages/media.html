Long bio

Alex Akinyele Adekunle is a Nigerian technology entrepreneur, web developer and business strategist. He founded Vavinix in 2021, a web design and digital branding company working with clients across the United States, the United Kingdom, Spain and Nigeria. He is also building Aspire Trybe, a movement for African tech talent, OneArtPiece, a marketplace for verified physical artwork, and The Receipt, a civic accountability platform auditing Nigeria’s thirty six state governors against their manifestos. He has been building on the web since 2011 and holds a BSc in Mathematics from the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta.
