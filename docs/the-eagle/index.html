The Eagle — a working philosophy

Rise abovethe noise.

Seven principles. Not a poster. A working philosophy I have to reread more often than I would like.

DisciplineFocusConsistencyFaithPatienceHustleFreedom
i

Discipline

Do what needs doing, especially on the days you do not want to. Motivation is weather. Discipline is climate.

01
ii

Focus

Most failure is not doing the wrong thing. It is doing nine things, all of them reasonably well.

02
iii

Consistency

Small actions repeated long enough become big results. There is nothing exciting about that, which is exactly why it keeps working for the few who do it.

03
iv

Faith

Believe in what you are building before anyone else can see it. Everything real spends a while existing only in one person’s head, and that stretch is lonelier than anyone tells you.

04
v

Patience

The internet makes it easy to mistake being seen for making progress. They are different things, and only one of them compounds.

05
vi

Hustle

Move. Learn. Adapt. Direction beats speed, but neither does much standing still.

06
vii

Freedom

A life where your time, your choices and your work belong to you. That is what the other six are for.

07

Why the eagle

Perspective.

Perspective. It sees further than the thing right in front of it and moves on purpose instead of in a panic.

Most of my bad decisions were made at ground level, fast, with something urgent shouting in my ear. The eagle is the reminder to climb before choosing. I manage it more often than I used to.
