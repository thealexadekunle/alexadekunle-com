Writing — essays, notes and principles

Thinking out loud,on purpose.

Essays when an idea deserves an argument. Notes when it just deserves writing down.

What I write about

  • Technology

    From a builder rather than a spectator, which mostly means I am less impressed than the timeline.

  • Business

    The unglamorous decisions that turn work into value.

  • Leadership

    The quiet responsible version rather than the loud photographed one.

  • Africa

    Where the talent has never been the constraint and access always has been.

Two kinds of piece

  • 01EssaysOne idea, argued properly, written to still be true in two years.Evergreen
  • 02NotesDated, specific, from inside whatever I am building right now. Lessons, experiments, what things cost, and the occasional thing I would do differently.Dated
  • 03Adekunle PrinciplesA running series of short principles from years of building, failing and starting again. One idea each, no padding.Series

Why I bother

Memory is selective and building moves fast. Ask me in two years how something went and you will get the tidy version, which is usually the wrong one.

Writing it down now keeps the real lesson.
